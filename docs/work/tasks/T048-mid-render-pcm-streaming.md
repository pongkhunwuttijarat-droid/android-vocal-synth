## Handoff notes

The final WORLD output sample is the sum of impulse responses centered at pulse locations. After processing pulse `i`, samples before the next pulse's window start are final. Emitting at that watermark preserves the existing offline algorithm; PhraseSynth buffers the final 10 ms so its fade remains identical. The callback is synchronous and can apply bounded backpressure. Callback errors abort the pulse loop.
